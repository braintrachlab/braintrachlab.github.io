About us

The lab

A research group dedicated to the anatomy and function of the brain's white matter, and to the history of the discipline that studies it.

Mission

Understanding how the brain is connected

The white matter makes up roughly half of the human brain. Its pathways connect distant regions into networks that support movement, perception, language, memory and behaviour. Describing these connections precisely is essential to understanding how the brain works, and what happens when it is damaged.

Our work focuses on the description of white matter tracts and their functions, in healthy subjects and in individuals with pathological conditions, with the aim of contributing both to basic neuroscience and to clinical practice.
