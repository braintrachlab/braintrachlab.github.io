term: Arcuate fasciculus
latin: Fasciculus arcuatus
synonyms: Superior longitudinal fasciculus (in part, in older texts)
system: Association
text: A perisylvian association tract that arches around the Sylvian fissure, connecting the temporal lobe with the frontal and parietal lobes. It is a key pathway of the language network.
history: Described by Reil at the beginning of the 19th century and named *fasciculus arcuatus* by Burdach (1822) for its arched course.
links: [Catani & Thiebaut de Schotten, *Cortex* (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cortex.2008.05.004)

term: Anterior commissure
latin: Commissura anterior
system: Commissural
text: A compact bundle crossing the midline in front of the fornix columns, connecting parts of the temporal lobes and the olfactory regions of the two hemispheres.

term: Anterior thalamic radiations
latin: Radiatio thalami anterior
synonyms: Anterior thalamic peduncle
system: Projection
text: Fibres linking the anterior and medial thalamic nuclei with the prefrontal and anterior cingulate cortex through the anterior limb of the internal capsule.

term: Cingulum
latin: Cingulum
system: Limbic
text: A long association tract running within the white matter of the cingulate gyrus, from the orbitofrontal region around the corpus callosum to the parahippocampal gyrus and the temporal pole.
history: The name, from the Latin for "girdle", was given by Burdach (1822).

term: Corpus callosum
latin: Corpus callosum
synonyms: Great commissure
system: Commissural
text: The largest commissure of the brain, linking the two cerebral hemispheres. Its parts are the rostrum, genu, body and splenium; the forceps minor and forceps major are its frontal and occipital radiations.
history: Known since antiquity and described by Galen; illustrated by Vesalius in the *De humani corporis fabrica* (1543).

term: Fornix
latin: Fornix
system: Limbic
text: The main output pathway of the hippocampus, arching under the corpus callosum to the mammillary bodies, the septal region and the anterior thalamus.

term: Frontal aslant tract
synonyms: FAT
system: Association
text: A short frontal tract linking the posterior inferior frontal gyrus with the supplementary and pre-supplementary motor areas, involved in speech initiation and verbal fluency.
history: Named in the tractography era, as one of the short frontal lobe connections of the human brain.
links: [Citro et al., *Nature Communications* (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73731-8)

term: Inferior fronto-occipital fasciculus
latin: Fasciculus fronto-occipitalis inferior
synonyms: IFOF, inferior occipito-frontal fasciculus
system: Association
text: A long ventral pathway from the occipital and posterior temporal cortex to the frontal lobe, passing through the temporal stem and the extreme/external capsule.

term: Inferior longitudinal fasciculus
latin: Fasciculus longitudinalis inferior
synonyms: ILF
system: Association
text: A ventral association tract connecting the occipital lobe with the anterior temporal lobe, along the lateral wall of the temporal horn of the lateral ventricle.

term: Uncinate fasciculus
latin: Fasciculus uncinatus
synonyms: UF
system: Association
text: A hook-shaped tract connecting the anterior temporal lobe with the orbitofrontal cortex through the limen insulae.
history: The name refers to its hooked course (Latin *uncus*, hook).
