Who we are

Anatomy as the foundation of brain function

TrachLab is a research group devoted to the description of the white matter connections of the human brain. By combining detailed anatomical study with the analysis of their functional role, we aim to understand how the brain's networks support behaviour and cognition, in healthy individuals and in people with neurological conditions.

Alongside this work, the lab cultivates a strong interest in the history of neuroscience, tracing how our knowledge of the brain's connections has taken shape over the centuries.

Research areas

What we study

i.

White matter anatomy

Detailed description of the course, organisation and terminations of the brain's association, projection and commissural pathways.

ii.

Connections & disease

How the disruption of white matter pathways relates to clinical symptoms in patients with neurological and psychiatric conditions.

iii.

History of neuroscience

The figures, ideas and discoveries that shaped our understanding of brain anatomy and connectivity, from early anatomists to the present.
