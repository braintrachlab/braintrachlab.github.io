Research

Our research

From the detailed anatomy of single pathways and their evolution across primates, to their role in disease and the history of how they were discovered.

Image
01

White matter anatomy

We describe the course, organisation and cortical terminations of the white matter tracts of the human brain, building an accurate anatomical map of its association, projection and commissural connections.

02

Comparative neuroanatomy

By comparing the white matter of the human brain with that of non-human primates, we study how connections have changed during evolution and how these changes relate to abilities such as language and vocal communication.

Image
Image
03

Connections in health and disease

By studying the functions of white matter pathways, we investigate how their damage or alteration contributes to the clinical picture of patients with neurological and psychiatric conditions.

04

History of neuroscience

The lab explores the history of brain anatomy and neuroscience: the scientists, methods and ideas that, over the centuries, led to our current understanding of the brain's connections.
