Research

Our research

01

White matter anatomy

We study the origin, course and terminations of the major association, commissural and projection pathways of the human brain, and how they form large-scale networks for movement, language and communication, social behaviour, emotion, memory and vision. Using tractography, we have also described white matter tracts previously unknown.

02

Comparative neuroanatomy

Tractography has made it possible, for the first time, to compare the anatomy of white matter quantitatively across species. We aim to identify the connections we share with our closest relatives, the non-human primates, and the evolutionary changes that underlie abilities unique to humans, such as language.

03

Connections in health and disease

In health, tractography shows how differences in white matter relate to individual abilities. In patients, from neurodevelopmental disorders to dementia, it reveals the anatomical basis of symptoms, and it has led us to describe new clinical syndromes caused by damage to specific white matter pathways.
