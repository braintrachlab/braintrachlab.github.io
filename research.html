Our research

White matter anatomy

We describe the course, organisation and cortical terminations of the white matter tracts of the human brain, building an accurate anatomical map of its association, projection and commissural connections.

Comparative neuroanatomy

By comparing the white matter of the human brain with that of non-human primates, we study how connections have changed during evolution and how these changes relate to abilities such as language and vocal communication.
